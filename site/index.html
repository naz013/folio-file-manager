Folio GitHub

A calm place for your files.

Folio is a lightweight, Finder-inspired file manager. Tabs, color tags, drag and drop, a space breakdown and cleanup that always goes through the Recycle Bin.

Download for Windows Windows installer

Made for people who live in their files

  • Fast

    Large folders scroll smoothly. Lists are virtualized, so size doesn't slow it down.

  • Tabs and color tags

    Keep several places open and mark files with colors you can find again.

  • Safe by design

    Deletes go to the Recycle Bin. Cleanup never removes anything permanently.

  • See where space goes

    A space breakdown shows which folders are heavy, before you clean up.

  • Light and dark

    Follows your system theme, with a design that stays out of the way.

  • More coming

    macOS and Linux follow Windows. See the roadmap.
